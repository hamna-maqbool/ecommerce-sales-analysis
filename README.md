## About this project
Analyzed 4 years of retail sales data from a US Superstore to identify 
top performing product categories, monthly sales trends, and regional 
profitability patterns.

## Business questions answered
- Which product category generates the highest sales?
- Which months have the highest sales volume?
- Which region is the most profitable?

## Key findings
- Technology category drives the highest total sales
- Sales peak significantly in Q4 (October to December)
- The West region generates the highest profit overall

## Tools used
- Python 3
- pandas — data cleaning and analysis
- matplotlib — data visualization
- Jupyter Notebook

## Files in this project
- `analysis.ipynb` — main analysis notebook
- `chart1_category.png` — sales by product category
- `chart2_monthly.png` — monthly sales trend by year
- `chart3_region.png` — profit by region

## Dataset
Superstore Sales Dataset from Kaggle — 9,994 rows of US retail orders
